Padel & Pickleball Score Keeper

A simple scoreboard for padel, pickleball and tennis: tap to score, golden point or advantage, tie-breaks, Americano and Mexicano tournaments, match history and CSV export.

Get the app: Google Play (Android) · App Store (iPhone) – coming soon

Common questions

How do I score? Tap a team's half to add a point. Swipe sideways or press UNDO to take the last point back. Rotate your phone for a landscape scoreboard.

Exporting history: History > Export creates a CSV file you can open in any spreadsheet app.

Your data

Matches and settings stay on your phone. No account is needed. Delete them in the app or by uninstalling it.
