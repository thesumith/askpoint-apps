Quiz progress

Quiz answers and study progress are stored on the device. Clear the app's storage in the system settings, or uninstall the app, to remove that record from the device. There is no account to sign in to.

iOS purchases

iOS premium purchases use StoreKit 2. The app restores purchases from the Apple ID and grants premium access from the current signed entitlements. The current iOS build does not request ads and does not send usage data to Firebase.

If Apple sold you an auto-renewing subscription, manage or cancel it in the device's Apple ID subscription settings. Apple's guide is Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Android purchases and ads

Android purchases use Google Play Billing. SkyReady Pro on Google Play is a weekly subscription with a 3-day free trial, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. Pro removes ads. The free experience uses Google AdMob for a small banner and an optional ad for bonus questions. Manage or cancel an Android subscription in Google Play. Reinstalling the app with the same Google account makes Play Billing purchases available again.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use. Those pages describe on-device study data, iOS StoreKit purchases, the current iOS build with advertising and Firebase turned off, and the Android use of Google Play Billing, AdMob, and Firebase.
