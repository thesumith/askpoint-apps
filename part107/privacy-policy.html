This policy covers the iOS and Android versions of this app. The two versions do not handle purchases, advertising, or analytics in the same way. The section for each platform is limited to behavior confirmed for that platform.

Disclaimer: Independent study app. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA. Study content is original and based on public-domain FAA publications and 14 CFR Part 107. Always verify with official sources at faa.gov/uas.

1. On-device quiz and progress

The app does not require an account or login. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details.

Quiz answers and study progress are stored on the device. On Android, bookmarks and the countdown to your test date are also kept on the device. The published Android app works offline and does not require an account.

You can remove the on-device study record by clearing the app's storage in the system settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. iOS purchases, ads, and analytics

Premium purchases on iOS are processed by Apple with StoreKit 2. The app grants premium access from the current signed App Store entitlements and can restore those purchases. Apple charges your Apple ID. We do not receive your payment card number.

The current iOS build does not request or display advertisements.

The current iOS build does not enable Firebase. Firebase stays off unless the build contains an explicit real Firebase configuration and the FIREBASE_IOS flag. Neither has been added to this build, so it does not send analytics or other usage data to Firebase.

Prices, durations, and any subscription terms for an iOS product are shown by the App Store before purchase. This policy does not set those terms.

4. Android purchases, ads, and analytics

Android purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. The Google Play listing for this app offers SkyReady Pro as a weekly subscription with a 3-day free trial, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. SkyReady Pro removes ads. We do not receive your payment card number. Purchase records for the Android app are handled with the Google account used on Google Play.

The Android app shows ads with Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase. On the free experience the Google Play listing describes a small banner and an optional ad that can be watched for bonus questions, and states that there are no pop-up interstitial ads.

Google Play's Data safety section for com.askpoint.part107, as published on 7 November 2026, says the Android app may:

  • collect approximate location, for analytics and for advertising or marketing;
  • collect app interactions, for analytics and for advertising or marketing;
  • collect device or other IDs, for analytics and for advertising or marketing;
  • share device or other IDs, for analytics and for advertising or marketing;
  • collect purchase history, for app functionality.

That is the complete set of categories declared there. This policy does not add other collection categories. Data safety also states that data is encrypted in transit and that you can request deletion. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, and control ad personalization, in the device settings. Google describes its own processing in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. On Android, sharing is limited to the categories and purposes declared in section 4. On iOS, purchase validation stays with Apple through StoreKit 2. Disclosure required by law is still possible.

7. Children

The app is a study aid for people preparing for the FAA Remote Pilot (Part 107) knowledge test, including adults and teens. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, email us and we will help delete the information we control.

8. Data retention and deletion

The on-device quiz and progress record remains on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

The current iOS build does not send Firebase analytics. Apple keeps Apple ID purchase records under Apple's policies. The Android analytics and advertising data described in section 4 is retained by Google under Google's policies. Email us to request deletion of information we can delete. We cannot delete records that Apple or Google hold in your store account; those companies provide their own account and privacy tools.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A build that turns on iOS advertising or iOS Firebase will not ship against this text; this page will be updated first.
