1. Information we collect

The app does not require an account or login and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or other personal contact details. Pay level, pay, pension and other values you enter are processed only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Your preferences (language, theme) are stored locally and can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

2. Configuration download

The app downloads a small public configuration file (current DA rate and default values) from GitHub Pages. This request contains no personal data; GitHub may log standard request data such as IP address under its own privacy policy.

3. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (e.g. screens viewed, app opens, chosen pay level and fitment factor, approximate region, device model, OS version, app instance ID). This data is aggregated and used to improve the app.

4. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs and diagnostic information (device state, OS version, stack traces, app instance ID) to fix bugs and improve stability.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the device's Advertising ID, IP address, and app interaction data to serve, measure and (where permitted) personalise ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads). See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics)

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Data collected by the services above is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS) and processed by Google under its own policies. We do not share data with any other parties except as required by law.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults (employees and pensioners). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you delete them. Analytics and crash data are retained per Google's default retention settings. To request deletion of data associated with your app instance, email us.
