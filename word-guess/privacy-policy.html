Privacy Policy

App: Word Guess: Daily Word Puzzle (com.askpoint.wordguess)
Developer: Askpoint, Hubballi, Karnataka, India
Effective date: 31 October 2026

1. Summary

Word Guess is a free word puzzle game supported by ads. It needs no account. Your game progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We use Google AdMob to show ads and may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. These services may collect your device's Advertising ID as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Your guesses for the current puzzles, statistics (games played, wins, streaks, guess distribution) and preferences (word length, hard mode, colour-blind mode, theme, haptics, reminder time) are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) and the Advertising ID

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the statistics and practice screens, an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to reveal a hint, and an occasional full-screen ad between practice games (never during the daily puzzle). AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier / AD_ID permission), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information and ad interaction data to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change their choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It logs events such as app opens, games started, won or lost, the word length played, number of guesses, hints used and shares, together with data collected automatically by Firebase (app instance ID, Advertising ID where available, device model, OS version, app version and approximate region). We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder per day on your device when the new daily puzzle is available. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in Android settings. Notifications are generated on your device and contain no ads.

6. Sharing results

When you tap Share, the app copies a text grid of coloured squares (no letters) and a link to the app to your clipboard and opens the Android share sheet. It is only sent to the app or person you choose.

7. Ratings

After a winning streak the app may show Google Play's in-app review prompt. Reviews are handled by Google Play.

8. Device permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load ads and send analytics.
  • Notifications – the optional daily puzzle reminder.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) – so the daily reminder is restored after a restart.
  • Vibration – haptic feedback.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) – used by AdMob and Firebase as described above.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, messages or files, and does not use exact alarms.

9. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob / Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics)
  • Google Play (in-app review)

10. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Data collected by the services above is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS) and processed under their own policies. We do not share data with other parties except as required by law.

11. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA / DPDP Act India)

Because your game data stays on your device, you control it directly. For data processed by Google you may withdraw ad consent in the app, reset or delete your Advertising ID, opt out of personalised ads, or contact us to exercise your rights of access, correction or deletion.

12. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Analytics data is retained according to Google's retention settings. To request deletion of data associated with your app instance, email us.

14. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
