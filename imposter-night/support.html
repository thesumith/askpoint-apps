Imposter Night: Party Game

A pass-the-phone party word game for 3 to 20 players. Everyone gets the secret word except the imposter, who has to bluff. Talk, vote, and catch the imposter before they guess the word. Works offline on one phone.

How to play

  1. Add the players' names (3 to 20) and pick word packs and a mode.
  2. Pass the phone. Each player taps to flip their card in private: everyone sees the secret word, except the imposter.
  3. Take turns saying one word or clue about the secret word. When the timer ends, vote for who you think the imposter is.
  4. If the imposter is caught, they get one last guess at the word to steal the round.

Common questions

Game modes: Classic (the imposter knows they are the imposter and can get a hint), Undercover (the imposter gets a similar decoy word and may not know it) and Chaos (a secret number of imposters). All modes are free.

Word packs on iPhone: all 24 packs (over 1,000 words) are included free in the iPhone version. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Word packs on Android: 10 packs are free; the bonus packs come with Imposter Night Pro (Google Play) or can be unlocked for a few hours by watching a video. Restore Pro from Settings > Restore purchases with the same Google account.

Seeing the same words? The app avoids repeats across games. Settings > Reset word history starts the word list over.

Your data

Player names, settings, scores and played words stay on your phone. No account is needed. Delete them in Settings or by uninstalling the app.
