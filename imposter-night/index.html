Privacy Policy

Support

App: Imposter Night: Word Party Game (com.askpoint.imposternight)
Developer: Askpoint, Hubballi, Karnataka, India
Effective date: 16 November 2026 (iPhone section added 24 November 2026)

1. Information we collect

The app does not require an account or login and does not ask for your email, phone number or other contact details. Player names you type in, your selected word packs, game settings, scores and the list of words already played are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. You can delete them by removing players, using "Reset word history" in Settings, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app. The game works fully offline.

2. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, for example app opens, games and rounds started or finished, game mode, number of players, paywall views and share taps. Firebase may collect the app instance ID, device model, OS version, approximate region (derived from IP address) and app interaction events. Player names and secret words are not sent. This data is aggregated and used to improve the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads, occasional interstitial ads between rounds and optional rewarded ads (watch a video to unlock bonus word packs for a few hours), served by Google AdMob. Pro users see no ads. AdMob may collect and use the device's Advertising ID, IP address, and app interaction data to serve, measure and (where permitted) personalise ads. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), we ask for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Imposter Night Pro (a one-time lifetime unlock, or an optional weekly or yearly subscription) is sold and processed by Google Play Billing. The app checks your Google Play purchases on the device to unlock Pro and to restore it on a new device. We never receive your card or payment details.

5. Sharing features

When you tap Share or Invite, the app creates an image (for example a Game Night Recap with player names and scores) or a text message and opens your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and confirm. What you share is handled by the app you share to.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics)
  • Google AdMob / Google Play / Google Privacy Policy

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Data collected by the services above is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS) and processed under those providers' own policies. We do not share data with any other parties except as required by law.

8. Children

Imposter Night is a general-audience party game for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it. Analytics data is retained per Google's default retention settings. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your app instance, email us.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

iPhone (iOS) version

The iPhone version of Imposter Night (effective 24 November 2026) does not collect any data. It does not show ads, does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, does not run Google Firebase Analytics and has no in-app purchases: all word packs are included free. Sections 2, 3 and 4 above apply to the Android version only. Player names, settings, scores and played words stay on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app. Sharing a round card or an invite only sends what you choose through the iPhone share sheet. If a later iPhone version adds ads, analytics or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your permission before any tracking.
