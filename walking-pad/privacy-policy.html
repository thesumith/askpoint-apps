Privacy Policy

App: Walking Pad Tracker – Treadmill & Step Counter (com.askpoint.walkingpad)
Developer: Askpoint, Hubballi, Karnataka, India
Effective date: 30 October 2026

1. Information we collect

The app does not require an account or login and does not ask for your email, phone number or other contact details. The profile details you enter (units, height, weight and optional sex), your daily goals, walking sessions (time, speed, distance, steps, calories, incline), streaks and preferences (theme, reminders) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. You can delete this data at any time by deleting sessions in the app, using Settings > Clear history, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

2. Step sensor and activity data

If you turn on Pocket mode, the app asks for the Android "Physical activity" (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) permission and reads your phone's built-in step counter sensor only while a walking session is running. Step counts and all sensor and activity data are processed and stored on your device only. They are never sent to us, to advertisers, to analytics or to any third party. Pocket mode is optional; if you do not grant the permission, the app estimates steps from your speed and height instead.

3. Exporting data

When you choose to export your history (CSV), the app creates the file on your device and hands it to the Android share sheet. It is only sent to the app or person you choose.

4. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (e.g. app opens, screens viewed, session started/saved events with duration and tracking mode, approximate region, device model, OS version, app instance ID). This data is aggregated and used to improve the app. Your height, weight, step counts and health-related details are not sent.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner ads, occasional full-screen ads after you save a session, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch to unlock themes or CSV export). Ads are never shown on the live session screen while you are walking. AdMob may collect and use the device's Advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to serve, measure and (where permitted) personalise ads. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent via Google's consent form and can change their choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Device permissions

The app uses Internet access (for ads and analytics), Physical activity / step counter (only for Pocket mode, as described above), notifications (only if you turn on the daily walk reminder), wake lock (to keep the screen on during a session), boot completed (to restore your reminder after a restart) and vibration (haptic feedback). It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

7. Health disclaimer

Distance, steps and calories shown in the app are estimates for general fitness tracking only. They are not medical advice and the app is not a medical device.

8. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics)
  • Google AdMob / Google Privacy Policy

9. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Data collected by the services above is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS) and processed by Google under its own policies. We do not share data with any other parties except as required by law.

10. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA / Australian Privacy Act)

Because your profile, sessions and step data stay on your device, you control them directly. For data processed by Google for ads or analytics you may withdraw consent in the app, reset your Advertising ID, or contact us to exercise your rights of access, correction or deletion.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it. Analytics data is retained per Google's default retention settings. To request deletion of data associated with your app instance, email us.

13. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
