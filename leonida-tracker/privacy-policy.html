Privacy Policy

App: Leonida Tracker – unofficial fan companion (com.askpoint.leonidatracker)
Developer: Inovasys Innovations (Askpoint apps), Hubballi, Karnataka, India
Contact: sumithsubhanjirao@gmail.com
Effective date: 20 November 2026

1. Summary

Leonida Tracker does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your cheat progress, favorites, explored regions and trivia streak are stored only on your device. We use Google AdMob to show ads and Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. These Google services may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

2. Data stored on your device

Cheat challenge counters, cheats you mark as unlocked, favorites, regions you mark as explored, your "story beaten" setting and trivia results (score, streak) are saved locally on your phone. They are never uploaded to our servers. You can erase them at any time with Settings > Reset all progress, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interactions to deliver, measure and (where you consent) personalize ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to see which features are used (for example: tab views, opening a cheat or answer, finishing a trivia round, searches by length only, favorites). Firebase may collect app-instance identifiers, device model, OS version, app version, country-level location and usage events. We do not send your search text or any personal information to analytics. See Firebase privacy information.

5. In-app purchase

If offered, the optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to verify the purchase; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt data. We never see or store your payment details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, for the purposes described. All data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Children

The app is a companion for a game rated Mature 17+ (ESRB) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local data stays until you reset it or uninstall the app. Analytics data is kept by Google according to Firebase's retention settings (up to 14 months). To ask questions or request deletion of any analytics data associated with your app instance, email us at the address above.

9. Unofficial fan app

Leonida Tracker is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto, GTA and related names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to describe what game this companion is for.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
