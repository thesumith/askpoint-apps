Askpoint CRM

The staff app for counsellors and managers at Askpoint School of Life Sciences, Hubballi: leads, follow-ups, admissions and reminders.

Get the app: Google Play (Android) · App Store (iPhone) – coming soon

Developer: Askpoint, Hubballi, Karnataka, India

Contact

Email sumith.rao@askpoint.in. Please include your phone model, whether it is iPhone or Android, the app version, and a short description of the issue. Never send a payment card number or a password.

Accounts

Accounts are created by Askpoint administrators; there is no public sign-up. Forgot your password? Ask your administrator.

Delete your account: in the app open Settings > Delete my account, or email us from your work address. The administrator permanently deletes the account and its sign-in, usually within 7 days and always within 30 days. Leads and records created for the institute stay with the institute.
