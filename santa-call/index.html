Santa Call: Message from Santa

Personalized voice messages and a surprise call from Santa, made by parents. Type your child's name, a good deed and their wish, and Santa says it in a warm, jolly voice. Share a Nice List certificate and count down to Christmas Eve.

Santa's voice

Santa's voice is made on your phone with its built-in text-to-speech voice, then lowered and warmed up by the app. It is an original, computer-generated character voice and does not imitate any real person.

iPhone: if a message can't be created, check Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English. Downloading an enhanced English voice can make Santa sound even better. The app plays sound even when the silent switch is on; check the volume buttons if you hear nothing.

Android: install or update "Speech Recognition & Synthesis" by Google from the Play Store.

Santa Magic Pack (one-time purchase)

The optional Santa Magic Pack is a one-time purchase that unlocks every template, unlimited messages and certificates, and removes ads. It is not a subscription. On a new or reset phone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Refunds are handled by the store: Apple or Google Play.

Your data

Names, wishes and messages you create stay on your phone. Delete them in My messages, with Settings > Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app. See the privacy policy for ads and analytics details.
