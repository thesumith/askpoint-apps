Privacy Policy

App: Santa Call – personalized Santa messages (com.askpoint.santacall)
Developer: Inovasys Innovations (Askpoint apps), Hubballi, Karnataka, India
Contact: sumithsubhanjirao@gmail.com
Effective date: 21 November 2026

1. Summary

Santa Call is an entertainment app for parents. Parents type a child's first name, age, a good deed and a wish-list item, and the app creates a voice message from Santa on the phone. No account is needed. What you type and the messages you create are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. We use Google AdMob to show ads to users of the free version and Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. These Google services may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

2. Information you type and messages you create

The first name, age, good deed, wish and optional extra line you enter, the audio messages and the certificates you create are saved locally on your phone only. Santa's voice is made with your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google's Speech Services). When the phone's engine uses an on-device voice the text never leaves the phone; if your phone is set to use an online voice, the text is processed by that engine's provider under its own privacy terms. We do not send what you type to analytics or advertising services. You can delete single messages in My messages, or everything with Settings > Delete all my data, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Sharing messages

When you tap Share, the audio file or certificate image is passed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email) through Android's share sheet. We do not receive a copy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob, only on parent-facing screens (never during message playback or the Santa call). AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interactions to deliver, measure and (where you consent) personalize ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform; you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying the Santa Magic Pack removes ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to see which features are used (for example: a message was created and which template, a call was started, a certificate was shared, the paywall was viewed). Firebase may collect app-instance identifiers, device model, OS version, app version, country-level location and usage events. Names, wishes and other text you type are never sent. See Firebase privacy information.

6. In-app purchase

The optional one-time "Santa Magic Pack" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app whether the pack was bought so it can unlock features.

7. Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, for the purposes described. All data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).

8. Children

Santa Call is designed for parents and adults, who set up messages and calls for their family. It is not directed at children, and children should not use it on their own. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; the child details a parent types stay on the parent's device.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Analytics data is kept by Google according to Firebase's retention settings (up to 14 months). To ask questions or request deletion of any analytics data associated with your app instance, email us at the address above.

10. Santa's voice

Santa's voice is an original, computer-generated character voice. It does not imitate any real person.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
